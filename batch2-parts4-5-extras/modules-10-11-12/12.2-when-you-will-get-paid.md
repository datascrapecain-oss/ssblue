# 12.2 When You Will Get Paid

## Your Payment Cycle

Most new UK sellers start on a 14-day rolling payment cycle. Your exact dates are shown in **Seller Central → Payments → Payments Dashboard**, along with a countdown to your next scheduled disbursement.

> 📸 **Screenshot needed:** the payout schedule / next disbursement date view

## From Disbursement to Your Bank Account

Once Amazon initiates the payout, it typically takes a few business days to actually clear into your linked account, Revolut Business, if you've set up your account the way this Blueprint recommends. The exact timing can vary slightly depending on your bank.

> 📸 **Screenshot needed:** a Revolut Business transaction showing an incoming Amazon payout landing, good full-circle image tying back to Module 3

## Your Very First Payout

Your first payout won't necessarily land exactly 14 days after your first sale, it lands at the end of whatever cycle your account is on when that sale happens. If your first sale lands a few days into a cycle, your first payout might be sooner than 14 days, or it might mean waiting through most of a full cycle. Check your Payments Dashboard rather than counting days from your first sale.

## As Your Account Matures

Amazon can move established accounts to a more frequent payout schedule over time. This isn't something you need to request or set up, it happens automatically as your account builds a track record.

## Completing This Blueprint

Your first payout is the final milestone in this Blueprint. Once it lands, upload your proof as covered in Module 13, and you'll unlock your free month of SourceSheets.
