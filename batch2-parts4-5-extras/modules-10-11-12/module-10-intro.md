# Module 10: Sending Your First Shipment to Amazon

Your stock has arrived (or is on its way), and it's tracked properly in your inventory tracker from Module 9. Now it needs to actually get to Amazon.

This module walks you through the real mechanics of an FBA shipment: how Amazon's inbound process works, how to create a shipment in Seller Central, how to label and package your stock correctly, and how to get it to Amazon, whether that's dropping it off yourself, booking a collection, or using a prep centre to handle it for you.

None of this is complicated once you've done it once. It just needs to be done in the right order.
