## Important caveat on sourcing

Unlike Module 9 or 11.4, I couldn't live-verify any of this against your actual Seller Central account, browser access was rate-limited earlier in this session and Module 10–12's content lives entirely in Amazon's own Seller Central UI, not SourceSheets, so it wasn't something the uploaded GitBook export could help with either. Everything here is written from how FBA shipping, inbound tracking, and payouts generally work for UK sellers: the shipment plan flow, FNSKU/poly-bag labelling rules, the UPS partnered-carrier discount, the 14-day rolling payout cycle, and so on.

Before this goes live, it's worth a pass to check against your current Seller Central screens specifically for:
- Exact menu paths (Amazon renames and reshuffles Seller Central navigation periodically)
- Your actual current payout cycle length (14 days is the common UK default but yours may differ)
- Whether "Stickerless, Commingled" is even still offered the way I described it
- The real UPS booking flow (drop-off locations, collection fee, exact screen names)

I flagged specific screenshot needs for all of this in the asset list (below) rather than guess at exact UI text, so when you're capturing shots you'll naturally be checking the flow matches what's written here too, and can flag me anything that's drifted.
