# Module 7.5: reduced screenshot structure

## What's changing

Each of the four Example subsections in 7.5 (Phases 2 through 5) currently shows 3 states × 2 tools (SAS + BBP) = 6 screenshots, 24 total across the module. With one built-in tool now, the tool duplication goes away on its own. I've also cut each phase from 3 states down to 2: one clean pass, one clear fail. That's 4 phases × 2 = 8 screenshots total.

Where a phase had two distinct fail modes (e.g. Phase 2 had "fails Gate 1" and "fails Gate 2" as separate examples), I picked the one that teaches the sharper lesson and left the other out, the gate's written explanation still covers both failure modes in prose, it just doesn't need a dedicated screenshot for each.

I checked the surrounding gate-definition text (Why Financial Gates Come First, the Gate 1/2/3 etc. explanations) while I was in there, it's written generically about profit, ROI, and demand, not tied to SAS or BBP by name. That prose should be fine as-is. I haven't read every single gate sub-page word for word though, so if the em-dash/AI-tell purge pass turns up a stray SellerAmp/BuyBotPro mention buried in one, flag it and I'll fix it on the spot.

## The 8-shot list

For each shot, use the ASIN Analyser on a real product and capture the panel named. Pick real products from your own account rather than test data, readers respond better to real numbers.

**Phase 2: Financial Reality Check (Gates 1–2)**
1. **Fail**: a product with weak profit or ROI after real fees. Capture the Profit Calculator panel showing the numbers falling short. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshots 1, 2, 4, 5.)
2. **Pass**: a product that clears both gates cleanly. Same panel, healthy numbers. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshots 3, 6.)

**Phase 3: Demand & Stability (Gates 3–4)**
3. **Fail**: a product with erratic price/rank history. Capture the Charts (Keepa-style) panel showing the volatility. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshot 3, 6 "Erratic Price and Rank Behaviour.")
4. **Pass**: a product with consistent sales and a stable rank line. Same panel. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshot 1, 4.)

*Dropped: the "short-term sales spike" example. Worth keeping as a written warning in the prose (a promo spike isn't real demand) even without its own screenshot.*

**Phase 4: Competition & Amazon Behaviour (Gates 5–6)**
5. **Fail**: a product where Amazon or a flood of sellers is suppressing price. Capture the Offers panel (seller count) alongside the price history. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshots 2, 3, 5, 6.)
6. **Pass**: a product with manageable, stable competition. Same panels. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshots 1, 4.)

**Phase 5: Risk Flags & Hard Stops (Gate 7+)**
7. **Fail**: a product with a real flag, hazmat/compliance is the clearest one to show. Capture the Recommendation & Risk panel with the flag visible. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshots 2, 3, 5, 6.)
8. **Pass**: a clean listing with no flags. Same panel. (Replaces old SAS/BBP Screenshots 1, 4.)

## One thing to flag

Screenshot 1 in this list technically has to demonstrate two different failure reasons at once (weak profit and weak ROI), which is a slightly bigger ask than the old SAS Screenshot 1/2 split. If you can't find one real product that cleanly fails both, two separate fail screenshots for Phase 2 (9 total instead of 8) is a fine trade, better a true example than a forced one.
