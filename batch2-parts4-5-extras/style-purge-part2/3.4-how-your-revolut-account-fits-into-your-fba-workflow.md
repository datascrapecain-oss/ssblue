# 3.4 How Your Revolut Account Fits Into Your FBA Workflow

Revolut Business isn't just another bank account, it slots directly into the way a new Amazon seller builds, grows, and manages their business. Here's why we recommend it, and how it fits into your workflow from day one.

## Clean Compatibility With Amazon Seller Central

Amazon is strict about banking. Revolut is one of the smoothest, most reliable accounts you can link to a new Seller Central account. It avoids the common issues sellers run into with traditional or newer banks: "bank not supported" errors, verification delays, and payout holds from mismatched details.

## Clear Separation of Business & Personal Spending

This Blueprint is built around proper business structure. Running your operation through Revolut gives you clean bookkeeping, an uncluttered transaction history, simple reconciliation, and fewer issues during Amazon audits. Everything stays organised from day one, which protects your account long term.

## In Short

Revolut keeps your account clean, compliant, organised, and fully Amazon-ready, while giving you better control over spending and faster support when you need it. It's a simple, seller-friendly foundation for the rest of your Amazon FBA journey.
