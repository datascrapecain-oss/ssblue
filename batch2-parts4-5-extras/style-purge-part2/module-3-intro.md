# Module 3: Opening Your Business Bank Account

## Why This Comes Next

With your Limited Company formed, you need a business bank account to trade through. This module walks you through opening and verifying your Revolut business account, the one we recommend as your primary trading account.

### What This Module Is About

You'll understand why we recommend Revolut, open your business account, verify it, and see how it fits into your day-to-day FBA workflow: paying suppliers, receiving Amazon payouts, and keeping your books clean.

### What You'll Get From This Module

By the end, you'll have a verified Revolut business account ready to connect to Amazon Seller Central, which you'll set up in the next module.

### Important

Keep your business banking separate from your personal accounts from day one. It makes bookkeeping, tax, and Amazon's own verification checks far simpler later on.
