## Step 7: Enter Your Full Legal Name

Tide needs your full legal name exactly as it appears on your passport or driving licence. This is passed directly to Companies House and will appear on the public record as part of your director appointment.

You'll see fields for first name, middle name(s), surname, and a checkbox for "I have a former name." Complete these exactly as shown on your ID.

Companies House cross-checks your details during incorporation. Using initials, shortened names, or nicknames can cause delays or failed submissions. If your passport includes middle names, enter them, even if you never use them day to day.

If you've changed your name (marriage, deed poll), tick "I have a former name" and Tide will give you a field to enter it. Most people leave this unticked.

## Step 8: Enter Your Date of Birth

Companies House requires this to appoint you as a director. Only the month and year appear on the public record; your full date of birth is used for identity verification only.

Enter it exactly as shown on your ID, or use the calendar icon to select the date. The Next button activates once it's entered correctly.

## Step 9: Enter Your Personal Address

Tide asks for your home address, starting with your postcode, for identity verification and director registration.

Your residential address does not appear on the public Companies House register. Only your director service address becomes public, and that comes later, with options to change or hide it. This screen is private and used only to confirm your identity and comply with UK KYC regulations.

Enter your postcode, select your address from the lookup, and click Next.

You'll then be asked how long you've lived there. If it's more than 12 months, select that option and click Next, nothing else is required. If it's less than 12 months, select that option and Tide will ask for your previous postcode and house number. This doesn't affect your LTD formation, Companies House simply needs a full 12-month address history.

## Step 10: Enter Your Country of Residence

This confirms where you currently live, part of the identity verification required by Companies House and UK banking regulations. Most UK-based sellers select United Kingdom, start typing "United" and it appears instantly. If you live abroad, choose the country where you're physically living.

This doesn't become public and doesn't affect your LTD formation unless you're resident in a restricted jurisdiction, which Tide would flag immediately.

## Step 11: Enter Your Nationality

Select your nationality exactly as it appears on your passport. For most UK users, this is British, but Tide supports the full international list. If you hold dual nationality, choose the one shown on your ID documents.

Unlike your address, phone number, and country of residence, your nationality is public. This is a legal requirement for all UK company directors and applies to every LTD in the country, so there's nothing to worry about.

## Step 12: Enter Your Security Information

Companies House requires three security questions. These are only used if you contact Companies House directly to verify your identity or update details manually. They're not used by Tide, SourceSheets, your bank, or Amazon.

Choose three questions with answers you'll remember and that won't change, such as birth town, mother's maiden name, father's first name, or passport number. Avoid anything that could change, like a current phone number.

These answers don't appear on the public record, aren't used by Tide for login or verification, and don't affect your bank account or Amazon account.

## Step 13: Enter Your Trading Information

This screen asks for your Trading Name and Trading Address. Your Trading Name is pre-filled with your LTD name (e.g. Alex Cain LTD). Leave it as is, you don't need a separate trading name for Amazon.

Your Trading Address is where your Tide Mastercard is sent, business correspondence may arrive, and some suppliers may verify your business. It's not the Companies House public address (that comes next), but it affects how smoothly your setup goes.

**Option 1: Use your personal address.** The simplest option, and what most new sellers choose. Your Tide card is sent home, your trading location is set to your home address, and none of this is public or shown to Amazon customers.

**Option 2: Add a new address (virtual office).** Optional, and it keeps your home address private while looking more professional to suppliers and wholesalers, who sometimes prefer a commercial address over a residential one. We work with 1st Formations, who offer virtual office addresses, mail forwarding, director service addresses, and registered office addresses at a low annual fee. If you want this later, Tide makes it easy to update your trading address and Companies House records.

If you're starting out and want to keep things simple, use your personal address. If you want a more professional presentation and full privacy, use a virtual address. Neither affects your ability to sell on Amazon.

## Step 14: Choose Your Registered Office Address

This is one of the most important screens in the formation process. Your Registered Office Address becomes visible on Companies House, appears on the public record, and receives legal notices and HMRC correspondence.

You do not need to pay Tide for a virtual office, you do not need a commercial address to start selling on Amazon, and you do not need to spend £15.83 + VAT a month. You can change your registered office later, cheaply and instantly. This step doesn't lock you into anything long-term.

**Option 1: Use your personal address (recommended for new sellers).** Most new sellers use their home address, or their trading address if it's different. This is completely legal, accepted by Amazon, HMRC, and Companies House, and free. It will make your address public on Companies House, which is normal for a new online business, and you can change it later.

**Option 2: Use your trading address.** For most new sellers this is the same as their personal address.

**Option 3: Add a new address (virtual office).** Skip this inside Tide, the price is too high. You'll see an offer for a virtual address at £15.83 + VAT a month through Tide's partner Hoxton Mix. You don't need this to form your company, and very few new sellers should pay this price.

For now, select "Use your personal address" or "Use your trading address." You can upgrade to a professional virtual office later once you're speaking to wholesalers or investing more heavily.

Tide asks for your business category, essentially your business type or sector. This doesn't lock you into a niche or restrict what you can sell. It's used for risk classification, banking compliance, and basic Companies House compatibility.

Select **Online Retailer / Online Shop / Ecommerce**. This covers Amazon selling, wholesale, online arbitrage, retail arbitrage, marketplace selling, and general online trading, and it's the broad, safe, accurate choice for new sellers.

This choice doesn't affect tax, doesn't limit what you can sell, and doesn't affect Amazon approval. It's not the SIC code, that comes next, and it's purely for banking and compliance.

## Step 17: Choose Your SIC Code

SIC codes are official classification codes used by Companies House and the UK Government to categorise your business activity. For Amazon FBA sellers, one code fits better than any other:

**47910: Retail sale via mail order houses or via Internet**

This covers Amazon FBA, online retail, e-commerce, marketplace selling, dropshipping, OA/RA/wholesale selling, and non-store retail. It's the correct and recommended code for all Amazon sellers.

You don't need an additional SIC code as a new seller. This one code covers online retail, e-commerce, marketplace sales, wholesale, retail arbitrage, and private label, every Amazon model. Additional codes are only needed if you run multiple distinct business activities (consulting, software development, property management), which Amazon sellers don't. Leave "Add an additional SIC code" unchecked.
