# 2.1 Why We Only Recommend Trading As A LTD Company

When starting Amazon FBA, people often say you can begin as a sole trader. You can, but it creates more problems than it solves. This Blueprint is designed to take you from zero to your first payout smoothly and safely, in a way that supports long-term growth. For that reason, we strongly recommend setting up a Limited Company (LTD) from day one.

## The Key Difference: You vs. The Business

As a sole trader, you are the business. If something goes wrong, a claim, a compliance issue, a financial dispute, it attaches directly to you and your personal finances.

With a limited company, the business becomes its own separate legal entity. You gain protection, credibility, and stability. It's the safer, cleaner way to start on Amazon.

## Trusted by Amazon, Suppliers, and Partners

Amazon reviews thousands of new accounts every week. Limited companies go through verification more smoothly and raise fewer risk flags, because they signal a more committed, structured business.

Wholesalers, financial partners, and our own preferred tools and accountants are built around limited company structures. Using a limited company aligns you with the systems that successful sellers rely on.

## Unlocking Better Financial Tools

Operating as an LTD opens the door to proper business banking, cashback cards, cleaner bookkeeping, and scalable tax structures. Most serious sellers eventually switch to an LTD. Starting correctly means you avoid that disruption later.
