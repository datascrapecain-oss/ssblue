# Module 4: Creating Your Amazon Seller Account

## Why This Comes Next

With your company formed and your business bank account open, you're ready to register with Amazon. This module walks you through creating and verifying your Seller Central account.

### What This Module Is About

You'll work through Amazon's registration and verification process step by step: your business details, identity checks, and linking the Revolut account you just opened.

### What You'll Get From This Module

By the end, you'll have a verified Amazon Seller Central account, ready to start using your SourceSheets setup to find and evaluate products in the modules that follow.

### Important

Amazon's verification can take anywhere from a day to a couple of weeks depending on how quickly your documents are approved. Have your ID and business documents ready before you start, and expect to wait on Amazon's side, not your own.
