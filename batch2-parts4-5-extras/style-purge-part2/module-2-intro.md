# Module 2: Setting Up Your Business

## Why This Comes First

Before you can trade on Amazon, you need a real, registered business behind you. This module walks you through forming your UK Limited Company, the foundation everything else in Part 2 builds on.

### What This Module Is About

You'll understand why we recommend trading as an LTD company from day one, then form your company through Tide, using our discounted rate and the reward for completing onboarding.

### What You'll Get From This Module

By the end, you'll have a registered UK Limited Company, ready to open business banking and create your Amazon Seller account in the modules that follow.

### Important

This step is quick, usually done within a day, but it unlocks everything else. Don't skip ahead until your company is formed.
