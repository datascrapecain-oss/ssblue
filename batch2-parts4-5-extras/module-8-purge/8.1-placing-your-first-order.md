# 8.1 Placing Your First Order (Retail & Online Arbitrage)

At this stage, you're not choosing a product yet, that's Module 7. This step is about what actually happens when you buy.

## Retail Arbitrage vs Online Arbitrage

**Retail Arbitrage (RA)** means buying in person, in a shop, exactly as any other customer would. You check the product against your gates using SourceSheets or the Amazon Seller app while you're stood in the aisle, then pay at the till and walk out with your stock.

**Online Arbitrage (OA)** means buying from a retailer's website instead of walking into a shop. You check the product the same way, then order it online and have it delivered, same as any online shop order.

Both are normal consumer purchases. There's no account to open, no application to submit, and no minimum order quantity, you're simply a customer buying a product you intend to resell.

## Checking Before You Buy

Whether you're in a shop or browsing online, the check is the same: run the product through the 7 Gates (Module 7) using SourceSheets, or scan the barcode with the Amazon Seller app if you're in-store and want a second opinion on restrictions and current buy box price. If it passes, buy it. If it doesn't, walk away, there's no cost to checking.

## Keep Your Receipt

Always keep the receipt or order confirmation. You won't need it for every product, but it's your proof of purchase if Amazon ever asks, and it matters if you later want to pursue ungating on a restricted brand (7.3 covers this).
