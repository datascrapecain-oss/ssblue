# 8.3 What Happens After You Place an Order

Once your order is placed on Qogita, your role shifts from doing to waiting. That's normal, and it's an important mental switch to make early on.

You'll see the order appear in your account straight away with an order number, supplier details, and a status indicator. From that point, Qogita manages the process for you.

## Order Processing (What "Normal" Looks Like)

Wholesale orders don't move at Amazon retail speed. It's common for an order to sit in a processing state for a few days while the supplier prepares it. That doesn't mean your order is stuck, something's gone wrong, or you need to chase anyone. It's just standard wholesale fulfilment.

## Shipping, VAT, and Charges

At checkout, shipping is included in the quoted price and VAT is shown separately. International orders may show VAT charged upfront.

## Invoices and Documentation

Your wholesale invoice is attached directly to your order once it's available. It's suitable for Amazon, can be downloaded anytime, and should be saved for future reference. You don't need to request paperwork manually. It's built into the process.

## The Key Takeaway

Once your order is placed, the hard part is over. Waiting is expected, and silence doesn't mean a problem. Your stock is moving through a normal wholesale process, exactly as intended.
