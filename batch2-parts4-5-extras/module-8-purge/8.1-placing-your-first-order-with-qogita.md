# 8.1 Placing Your First Wholesale Order (And Why We Start Here)

At this stage, you're not placing an order yet. This step is about making sure you're fully set up with a wholesale supplier, so when you're ready to buy, there are no technical or administrative blockers.

## Finding a Wholesale Supplier

A wholesale supplier connects verified sellers with brands, distributors, and authorised wholesalers. Look for clear wholesale pricing, proper Amazon-compatible invoices, a straightforward checkout, and order tracking you can actually follow. SourceSheets surfaces supplier stock through the Sourcing Library and Radar, so you're choosing from products already matched to Amazon listings, not searching blind.

## This Is Not a Sourcing Lesson

This section is about access and readiness, not product selection. Your goal here is to create and verify your account with a supplier, understand where orders, invoices, and statuses appear, and be fully ready to place an order once you've chosen a product.

Once your account is set up, you move to one of two paths.

## Your Next Step (Two Options)

**Option 1: Book a call with us.** Many members choose a short call where we help confirm a suitable first product, walk through the order placement live, and make sure quantities, pricing, and checkout are correct. This is the fastest and most confidence-building route.

**Option 2: Use the sheets and execute independently.** If you're comfortable, head back to the SourceSheets tools, apply the analysis skills you've learned, identify a suitable first product, and place the order yourself.

Both routes are equally valid. The goal isn't speed. It's correct execution. Once this step is complete, you're positioned to make your first real wholesale purchase, with support available at every stage.
