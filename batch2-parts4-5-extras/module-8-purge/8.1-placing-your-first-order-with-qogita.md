# 8.1 Placing Your First Order with Qogita (And Why We Start Here)

At this stage, you're not placing an order yet. This step is about making sure you're fully set up on the supplier we recommend for your first purchase, so when you're ready to buy, there are no technical or administrative blockers.

## What Qogita Is

Qogita is a UK- and EU-based wholesale marketplace connecting verified sellers with brands, distributors, and authorised wholesalers. For new Amazon sellers, it offers clear wholesale pricing, proper Amazon-compatible invoices, a clean, beginner-friendly platform, and straightforward checkout and order tracking. It removes unnecessary friction from your first wholesale experience.

## Why SourceSheets Recommends Qogita First

We start with Qogita because it lets you focus on process and confidence, not complexity. It's easy to navigate, transparent with pricing and documentation, and well suited to first-time wholesale orders.

SourceSheets also has a close working relationship with Qogita and operates as one of their ambassadors, meaning faster resolution if something looks unclear, fewer onboarding issues for new sellers, and the ability for us to actively support you through your first order. That makes Qogita the safest and most practical place to begin.

## This Is Not a Sourcing Lesson

This section is about access and readiness, not product selection. Your goal here is to create and verify your Qogita account, understand where orders, invoices, and statuses appear, and be fully ready to place an order once you've chosen a product.

Once your account is set up, you move to one of two paths.

## Your Next Step (Two Options)

**Option 1: Book a call with us.** Many members choose a short call where we help confirm a suitable first product, walk through the order placement live, and make sure quantities, pricing, and checkout are correct. This is the fastest and most confidence-building route.

**Option 2: Use the sheets and execute independently.** If you're comfortable, head back to the SourceSheets tools, apply the analysis skills you've learned, identify a suitable first product, and return to Qogita to place the order yourself.

Both routes are equally valid. The goal isn't speed. It's correct execution. Once this step is complete, you're positioned to make your first real wholesale purchase, with support available at every stage.
