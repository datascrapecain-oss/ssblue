# 8.4 From Buying to Building

Placing your first wholesale order is a genuine milestone. It's the point where you stop preparing and start operating as a seller.

Up to now, the focus has been on learning systems, understanding risk, and making sure everything is set up correctly. This chapter closes with action taken: you've committed capital, engaged a real supplier, and started the flow of stock into your business.

From here, there's no rush. You can wait for your order to arrive and send it into Amazon as a single shipment, or place additional orders so you have more stock ready to go at once. Both approaches are valid, the right choice depends on your confidence, budget, and how you want to structure your first inbound.

What matters is that the process is now familiar. You know how ordering works, what to expect, and that you're supported if you need help.

The next stage is about what happens after stock exists: preparing it, joining listings, and sending it into Amazon Seller Central correctly. That's where this Blueprint goes next.

Take a moment to acknowledge the progress you've made. This is where many people stop. You've moved past that point. When you're ready, and you've received your order, we move on to turning stock into sales.
