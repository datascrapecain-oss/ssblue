# 1.3 How the Milestones & Rewards Work

To make your journey clear and rewarding, this Blueprint is built around Milestones. A Milestone is a key step in your progress, something that moves your Amazon business forward in a meaningful way. Every time you complete one, you're closer to becoming a fully trading seller.

### What a Milestone Looks Like

Examples of Milestones you'll complete:

* Forming your LTD company
* Opening your business bank account
* Activating your Amazon Seller Account
* Evaluating your first product
* Placing your first order
* Sending your first shipment
* Receiving your first payout

Each Milestone includes clear instructions, simple verification, and support whenever you need it.

### Why Milestones Matter

You always know what to do next, there's no guessing or overwhelm, and small wins build real momentum. Instead of trying to learn everything at once, you stay focused on one small step at a time.

### Your Reward for Completing All Milestones

When you complete every Milestone and receive your first Amazon payout, you unlock a free extra month of SourceSheets. This gives you more runway to grow, reinvest, and scale with confidence.

We verify your Milestones, confirm your payout, and apply your reward automatically.

### What You Don't See Here

You won't see pressure to rush, complicated requirements, or hidden conditions. This system is designed to help you succeed, not stress you out.

Your job is simple: complete each Milestone, check in with us to let us know how you're getting on, and keep moving forward.

### Final Summary

Milestones create a clear path. Rewards keep you motivated. Support keeps you moving.

By the time you complete them all, you won't just understand Amazon FBA. You'll be a real seller with real stock, real sales, and real profit.
