# 14.1 Setting Up a Wholesale Supplier Account

At this stage, you're not placing an order yet. This step is about making sure you're fully set up with a wholesale supplier, so when you're ready to buy, there are no technical or administrative blockers.

## Finding a Wholesale Supplier

A wholesale supplier connects verified sellers with brands, distributors, and authorised wholesalers. Look for clear wholesale pricing, proper Amazon-compatible invoices, a straightforward checkout, and order tracking you can actually follow. SourceSheets surfaces supplier stock through the Sourcing Library, so you're choosing from products already matched to Amazon listings, not searching blind.

## Setting Up Your Account

Your goal here is to create and verify your account with a supplier, understand where orders, invoices, and statuses appear, and be fully ready to place an order once you've chosen a product. Most wholesale suppliers will ask for basic business details (your LTD company name and registered address) before approving an account, since they're selling to businesses, not consumers.

## Your Next Step (Two Options)

**Option 1: Book a call with us.** Many members choose a short call where we help confirm a suitable first product, walk through the order placement live, and make sure quantities, pricing, and checkout are correct. This is the fastest and most confidence-building route.

**Option 2: Use the sheets and execute independently.** If you're comfortable, head back to the SourceSheets tools, apply the analysis skills you've learned, identify a suitable product, and place the order yourself.

Both routes are equally valid. The goal isn't speed. It's correct execution. Once this step is complete, you're positioned to make your first wholesale purchase, with support available at every stage.
