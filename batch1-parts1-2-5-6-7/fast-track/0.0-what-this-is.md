# The Fast-Track

No essays, just the steps.

This is the whole path from zero to your first payout, condensed to what you actually need to do. If you want the full reasoning behind any step, the detailed guide is one click away on every page below.

Five steps, start to first payout:

1. **Setup**: register your company, open your business bank account, and get your Amazon Seller account live and verified
2. **Tools**: get SourceSheets configured to your own numbers, so every product you look at is filtered against real thresholds, not guesswork
3. **The 7 Gates**: how to judge a product in under a minute, and know when to walk away from one
4. **Buy, Ship, Track**: turn a decision into stock sitting in an Amazon warehouse, correctly labelled and ready to sell
5. **Sell & Get Paid**: what happens after you're live, from your first sale through to your first payout landing in your account

Work through them in order. Each one is short enough to finish in a sitting, and each depends on the one before it.
