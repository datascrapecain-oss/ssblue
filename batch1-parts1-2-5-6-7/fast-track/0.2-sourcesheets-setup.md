# 0.2 SourceSheets Setup

Two things to sort before you look at a single product: set your numbers so SourceSheets filters correctly, then know which screen to reach for and when.

### Set your numbers first

**Settings → Preferences.** Set these four before you look at a single product:

- [ ] Minimum Profit, Minimum ROI, Minimum Sales/mo (your default filters everywhere in SourceSheets). We recommend £1 profit, 20% ROI (Return On Investment), and 50spm (Sales Per Month) as a solid starting point.
- [ ] VAT status (Not Registered / VAT Registered, get this right, it changes every profit figure you see). 99% of new sellers will be "Not Registered". VAT is required after £90,000 in sales.
- [ ] Display mode (Card or Table, whichever you read faster). Card gives a bit more space to understand.
- [ ] Eligibility checks set to Bulk checks. This helps find what you can, and can't sell.

### Know where products actually come from

SourceSheets gives you four ways to find products. You'll likely end up using more than one:

- **Radar**: a single live feed aggregated across every supplier SourceSheets tracks, already filtered to your saved thresholds. The fastest way to see what's profitable right now, no setup needed.
- **Sourcing Library**: browse by source type (Retail Arbitrage, Online Arbitrage, Wholesale) or by Category, supplier by supplier, if you want to work a specific retailer's full range.
- **Picked for you**: a personalised feed (SourceScore) that learns from your Buy List and buying pattern. Thin to start with, gets sharper the more you use SourceSheets.
- **Analyse**: upload your own supplier price list (CSV or XLSX) and SourceSheets matches it to Amazon listings for you. Use this once you have a specific supplier's list SourceSheets doesn't already track.

### Know the single-ASIN tool

**ASIN Analyser** is for checking one specific product on demand, not for finding new ones. Paste an ASIN, get the full profit and competition breakdown. Use it to sanity-check something you've already spotted, not as your main sourcing feed. This gives full insight into the deeper data of a product, similar to standalone tools, without you paying another subscription!
