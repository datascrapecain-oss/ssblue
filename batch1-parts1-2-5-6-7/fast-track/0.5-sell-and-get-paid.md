# 0.5 Sell & Get Paid

This is the part most new sellers have the least visibility into ahead of time: what actually happens between a shipment landing at Amazon and money landing in your account.

### After your shipment arrives

- [ ] Watch for fulfillable status (24-72h normal, 10-14 days with no movement means open a case)

### Your first sale

- [ ] First sale timing varies, don't panic inside the first week
- [ ] Add your live ASIN to **ASIN Monitor** so you get alerted if price, competition, or eligibility changes rather than having to check by hand

### Getting paid

- [ ] Payouts run on a 14-day cycle from account activation, check your exact date in Seller Central's Payments dashboard

> 📸 **Screenshot needed:** Payments Dashboard or a payout notification
