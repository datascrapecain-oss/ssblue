# 0.3 The 7 Gates

The single most important page in the fast-track. One line per gate, then the decision.

1. **Profit After Real Fees**: after every fee, is there real profit left, not just a positive number
2. **ROI Before Optimism**: is the return worth the cash tied up
3. **Sales Velocity Consistency**: is it actually selling, consistently, not just once
4. **Rank Behaviour Over Time**: is rank and price stable, or erratic
5. **Amazon's Role in Pricing**: is Amazon on the listing suppressing price
6. **Offer Count Pressure**: how many sellers, and are they racing to the bottom
7. **Risk Flags & Hard Stops**: hazmat, compliance, restrictions, brand issues

### Decision

Reject is the default. Proceed Cautiously only if every gate passes cleanly. Park & Monitor if it's close but not there yet. Never "strong buy."

### Where this shows up in SourceSheets

Add a product to your **Buy List** and SourceSheets buckets it for you automatically: **Strong Opportunity**, **Need Review**, or **Rejected**. That bucket is the platform doing this same gate check for you. Use it as a first pass, then apply your own judgement on anything landing in Strong Opportunity or Need Review before you actually buy. The bucket is a starting point, not a substitute for reading the panels yourself.
