# 0.4 Buy, Ship, Track

Three stages between deciding to buy and having stock live on Amazon. Each one hands off to the next.

### Buy

Placing the order is the easy part. Keeping SourceSheets in sync with what you actually bought is what makes the rest of this run smoothly.

- [ ] Set up your account with your chosen wholesale supplier and place your first order
- [ ] Confirm every unit you ordered is on your **Buy List** in SourceSheets, quantity and cost matching your actual order

### Get it inventory-ready

SourceSheets can automatically tell Amazon what stock you're sending into their fulfilment centre. Set your SKU format once and every future send to Amazon inventory uses it automatically, no typing SKUs by hand product by product.

- [ ] Set your SKU format once, in **Settings → SKU Format**. Build it from the supplier, cost, and ASIN tokens, SourceSheets checks it fits Amazon's 40-character limit for you
- [ ] Send your Buy List products to your Amazon inventory using that SKU format, rather than typing SKUs by hand
>A SKU is basically a nickname given to a product, to help it be identified through its journey.

### Ship

This is where the product actually leaves your hands and heads to an Amazon fulfilment centre.

- [ ] Create the shipment in Seller Central, label every unit (FNSKU) as needed. Amazon might ask for some items to be bagged if they are liquid, or can spill.
- [ ] Ship via Amazon's partnered carrier, either UPS, or DPD, by arranging courier collection from your door!
