# 0.1 Setup Checklist

Seven steps, and they're sequential: your Amazon application needs the company and bank account to already exist, and SourceSheets needs Amazon linked before it can give you accurate numbers. Don't skip ahead.

**Company and banking**

- [ ] Register UK LTD via Tide (£14.99, SIC code 47910, claim £20 back)
- [ ] Open Revolut Business, verify, order card, top up and spend £60+

**Amazon Seller account**

- [ ] Register Amazon Seller Central: business details must match Companies House exactly
- [ ] Have ready: ID + proof of address (PDF, under 90 days old, direct from provider)
- [ ] Complete video verification call
- [ ] Link Revolut card, confirm Professional plan (£25 first month)

**Connect it to SourceSheets**

- [ ] In SourceSheets, go to **Settings → Connected Accounts** and link your Amazon Seller Central account. Eligibility checks and fee calculations everywhere in the platform get more accurate once this is linked, not just estimated.
