# 0.1 Setup Checklist

Eight steps, and they're sequential: your Amazon application needs the company and bank account to already exist, and SourceSheets needs Amazon linked before it can give you accurate numbers. Don't skip ahead.

**Company and banking**

- [ ] Register your UK LTD via our Tide link (👉 [https://sourcesheets.co.uk/recommends/tide](https://sourcesheets.co.uk/recommends/tide)) — £14.99 instead of £50 through Companies House, SIC code 47910, £20 back once you spend £100 through the account
- [ ] Open Revolut Business via our link (👉 [https://sourcesheets.co.uk/recommends/revolut](https://sourcesheets.co.uk/recommends/revolut))

**Amazon Seller account**

- [ ] Register Amazon Seller Central: business details must match Companies House exactly, including your registered address
- [ ] Have ready: photo ID + proof of address (PDF, under 90 days old, issued directly by the provider, not a screenshot)
- [ ] Complete the video verification call once Amazon books it
- [ ] Link your Revolut card as the payment method and confirm the Professional plan (£25 first month)

**Connect it to SourceSheets**

- [ ] In SourceSheets, go to **Settings → Connected Accounts** and link your Amazon Seller Central account. Eligibility checks and fee calculations everywhere in the platform get more accurate once this is linked, not just estimated.
