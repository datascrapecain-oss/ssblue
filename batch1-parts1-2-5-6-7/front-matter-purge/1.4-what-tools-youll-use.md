# 1.4 What Tools You'll Use (Top-Level Overview)

To run a simple, successful Amazon FBA business, you only need a small handful of tools, and we introduce them exactly when you need them, not before. Here's the top-level overview.

## SourceSheets

The core of your sourcing system. You'll use it to find real product opportunities, check profitability, filter out bad products quickly, access guided daily leads, and use tools like Ungate Search, Find a Product, Qogita, and more. SourceSheets gives you clarity, not overwhelm, and it's where most sellers find their first winning products.

## ASIN Analyser

This tells you everything you need to know about a product: whether you can sell it, how fast it sells, how profitable it is, who else is selling it, whether you're gated, and the full price and rank history. It's built into SourceSheets, so there's no separate tool to set up. It helps you avoid mistakes and make confident buying decisions.

## Amazon Seller Central

Your Amazon management hub. You'll use it to create shipments, add products, track inbound stock, view sales, read performance notifications, and see your payout schedule. You'll only use a few areas of it at first, we show you exactly where to click.

## Tide (Company Formation & Backup Bank)

We use Tide exclusively for forming your LTD at a discounted rate, receiving your £20 reward for completing their onboarding, and automatically opening a basic business account. This Tide account isn't your main trading bank, it just handles your company formation quickly and easily, and gives you a backup bank account if you ever need one.

## Revolut Business (Your Trading Bank)

We're partnered with Revolut, who offer support and guidance to new sellers. Your business runs through Revolut for clean business banking, smooth Amazon compatibility, easy payouts, and direct SourceSheets support. You'll set this up early so you can trade properly and avoid future issues.

## Qogita (Your First Wholesale Supplier)

Qogita gives new sellers instant access to wholesale products, £100 credit with your SourceSheets code, fast delivery, consistent stock, and fewer Amazon complaint risks. This is where many sellers place their first real order.

## A Simple, Beginner-Friendly Stack

You don't need 10 apps or complicated dashboards, just these essentials, introduced at the right time: SourceSheets to find opportunities, the ASIN Analyser to analyse safely, Seller Central to ship and sell, Revolut to run your business, and Qogita to place your first order.

As you grow, we'll show you optional extras like repricers (PPP, SellerFuse), inventory and profit trackers (Sellerboard, SellerToolKit), and accounting partners (RTAccounting).

But right now, this is all you need, nothing more.
