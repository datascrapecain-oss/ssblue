## What is SourceSheets?

SourceSheets is an all-in-one sourcing platform built to help Amazon sellers find profitable products faster, with less risk, and without wasting time. At its core, it removes the hardest part of Amazon FBA: finding products that actually sell and make money.

### What We Do

We combine data, automation, and real seller workflows into one system:

- **Daily Sourcing Sheets**: pre-analysed product leads with profit, ROI, and sales data
- **Custom Scanner**: upload any supplier or wholesale list and get instant deal analysis
- **Retail & Wholesale Coverage**: dozens of UK retailers and suppliers scanned daily
- **Ungating Support Tools**: find products specifically to unlock restricted brands and categories
- **Advanced Sourcing Tools**: EU flips, price tracking, and extended sourcing utilities

Everything is designed to save hours of manual work and let you focus on buying and scaling.
