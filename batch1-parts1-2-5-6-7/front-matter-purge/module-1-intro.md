# Module 1: Welcome to SourceSheets

## Welcome to Your Starting Point

This is where your journey begins. Whether you're completely new to Amazon FBA or you've been looking for a clearer way to get started, you're now in the right place. This module gives you a solid understanding of the system you're about to follow, before you take any action.

### What This Module Is About

Before setting anything up, it's worth understanding how this Blueprint is structured, what your journey will look like from start to finish, how progress is tracked through milestones, and which tools you'll be using and why. This removes confusion early and makes everything that follows far easier.

### What You'll Get From This Module

By the end, you'll understand exactly how the Blueprint works, know what your path to your first payout looks like, be clear on how progress and rewards are structured, and have a simple overview of the tools you'll use. You won't be doing anything technical yet, this is about clarity and direction.

### Important

Don't worry if everything doesn't click instantly. You're not expected to understand every detail right now. Focus on the big picture, the step-by-step structure will guide you through the rest.
