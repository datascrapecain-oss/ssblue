# Part 1: Orientation & Confidence

## Getting You Set Up the Right Way

Before you start buying products or using tools, you need clarity. This section gives you exactly that.

Part 1 covers: what you're building, how the process works, what your journey will look like, and which tools you'll actually use, and which you won't, yet.

### What This Section Covers

You'll be guided through why this Blueprint works and why most new sellers struggle without structure, your full journey from zero to first payout so you can see the end goal clearly from the start, how Milestones and rewards work, and the core tools you'll be using, introduced simply, without overwhelm.

### Your Goal in Part 1

By the end of this section, you should understand the full path ahead, know what each stage of the journey looks like, feel confident about where to start, and be ready to move into the practical setup stages. This is about confidence, not action, yet.

### Important

Don't rush this section. You don't need to memorise everything, just understand the flow. Once the structure makes sense, everything that follows becomes much easier.
