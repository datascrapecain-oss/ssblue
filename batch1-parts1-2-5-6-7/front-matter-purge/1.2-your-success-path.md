# 1.2 Your Success Path: From Zero to First Payout

This Blueprint gives you a simple, proven path from no business, no Amazon account, no experience, to your first products purchased, shipped, sold, and your first payout received.

## Here's What Your Journey Looks Like

**Build your business.** Start by putting the foundations in place: your LTD company, your business bank account, your Amazon Seller Account, and the small number of tools you'll actually use. Once these are in place, you're officially ready to begin.

**Learn the basics & evaluate products.** Next, learn how to identify profitable products using the right data: what a "good" product looks like, how to read the charts and sales data, how to check restrictions and ungating, and how to use SourceSheets to filter opportunities fast. This gives you the skills to make safe, confident buying decisions.

**Make your first purchase.** Using SourceSheets leads, your evaluation skills, and support from our staff, you'll find your first real opportunity. Then place your first order (we're partnered with Qogita to give you £100 credit towards your first stock), add the products to your Amazon inventory, and start tracking everything properly. This is the moment you become an active seller.

**Send your first shipment to Amazon.** Once your products arrive, you label and prepare your items, create your shipment in Seller Central, drop off with UPS or arrange collection, and Amazon receives, checks in, and stocks your products. Your store goes live the moment your products become fulfilable.

**Make sales & get your first payout.** When your products go live, you get your first sales, track your performance, and Amazon schedules your payout. Once your first payout lands, you've officially completed the Blueprint, and you unlock your free extra month of SourceSheets (we verify your Milestones and add it automatically). You've gone from zero to a fully trading Amazon seller.

## Summary

1. Set up your business
2. Activate your Amazon account
3. Learn the basics
4. Evaluate products
5. Place your first order
6. Send your first shipment
7. Make your first sale
8. Receive your first payout

A clear, simple journey, and you'll be supported at every step.
