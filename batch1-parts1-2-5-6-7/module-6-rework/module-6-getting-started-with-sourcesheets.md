# Module 6: Getting Started With SourceSheets

You've got the ASIN Analyser configured. Now it's time to actually find products.

SourceSheets gives you several ways in, and it's easy to open the dashboard for the first time and not know where to look first. This module fixes that: what each part of SourceSheets does, how to read a sheet without getting lost in columns, how to open a single product and actually understand what you're looking at, and how to say no to most of what you see, quickly and without guilt.

By the end of this module you'll be able to sit down, open SourceSheets, and work through real products with confidence.
