# 13.1 What Counts as "Completed Blueprint"

The Blueprint is considered complete once every Milestone along the way has been hit. If you've followed the modules in order, you've already done all of it, this page is really just a checklist to confirm nothing's been missed.

## The Full Milestone List

- **LTD Company Formed**: your business is legally registered (Module 2)
- **Revolut Business Bank Complete**: your trading account is active (Module 3)
- **Amazon Seller Account Fully Activated**: your Seller Central account is verified and live (Module 4)
- **First product evaluated**: you've run a product through the 7 Gates and made a genuine buy/reject decision (Module 7)
- **First order placed**: you've purchased real stock from a supplier (Module 8)
- **First shipment sent**: your stock has reached Amazon and gone fulfilable (Modules 9–10)
- **First sale made**: a customer has actually bought from you (Module 11)
- **First payout received**: Amazon has paid that revenue into your business bank account (Module 12)

## If You're Missing One

If you've completed everything above except the final payout, you're not behind. You're simply waiting on Amazon's payout cycle, which is normal and expected. If you're missing an earlier step, it's worth going back to that module rather than skipping ahead, each one is a genuine prerequisite for the next.
