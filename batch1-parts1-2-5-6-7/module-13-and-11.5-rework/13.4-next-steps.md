# 13.4 Next Steps

Completing the Blueprint isn't the end of the work. It's the point where the real business starts. Here's where to go from here.

## Keep Sourcing

The 7 Gates model from Module 7 doesn't stop applying after your first product. Every future purchase should go through the same process: reject far more than you accept, treat a pass as "not obviously bad" rather than "guaranteed," and never skip a gate because a product feels exciting. The sellers who last are the ones who keep applying this discipline long after their first sale.

## The SourceSheets Knowledgebase

Beyond this Blueprint, SourceSheets maintains an ongoing knowledgebase covering topics that go beyond a brand-new seller's first weeks: scaling order volume, VAT registration once you approach the threshold, expanding into new categories, and the tools that become worth adopting as you grow (repricers, inventory and profit trackers, and so on, covered generally in Module 11).

## Building Workflows

As your product count grows, the manual process that worked for one or two products won't scale on its own. Start noticing where you're repeating the same manual steps, checking prices, reordering stock, tracking shipments, that's usually the signal a tool or a small process change is worth adopting.

## Consistency Over Intensity

The biggest driver of long-term success in this business isn't a single great find. It's showing up and sourcing consistently, week after week, using the same discipline you built completing this Blueprint. Momentum compounds. A seller who evaluates a handful of products every week for a year will consistently outperform someone chasing one big win.
