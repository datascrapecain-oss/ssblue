# 13.2 Upload Payout Proof

Once your first payout has actually landed in your Revolut Business account, the last step is letting us know, so we can verify it and apply your reward.

## What to Submit

A screenshot showing your first Amazon payout landing, either from your Seller Central Payments Dashboard or from the incoming transaction in Revolut. Either is fine, what matters is that it clearly shows a real payout has been received.

## Where to Submit It

Submit this through your Milestones tracking inside SourceSheets, the same place you've been ticking off each Milestone as you complete it. Uploading your proof there flags it for verification.

> 📸 **Screenshot or short video needed:** submitting payout proof through the Milestones tab in SourceSheets. This is the one piece of this module that could not be fully verified against the live product: worth double-checking the actual submission mechanism matches what's written before this goes live.

## Why We Ask for Proof

This reward is genuinely funded by real sellers completing the Blueprint, so we verify each payout to keep it fair for everyone. It's a quick check on our end, not a barrier.
