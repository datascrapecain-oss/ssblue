# Case Study 2: Boring, Stable, and Profitable

This product doesn't stand out at first glance. Buy cost is £9.60, it sells for £14.99, and after fees that leaves £2.00 profit per unit at a 21% ROI. Both numbers clear the Gate 1 and Gate 2 floors, but only just, there's no dramatic upside and no illusion of a breakout opportunity. The numbers don't rely on perfect timing or favourable assumptions, they just work.

## What the Numbers Look Like

Sales volume sits around 85 units a month, clear of the Gate 3 floor, with steady monthly movement rather than spikes, creating predictable cash flow rather than short bursts of performance.

## Demand and Pricing Behaviour (Gates 3–4)

Sales velocity is stable and repeatable across the last 30 days. Rank moves in a narrow, understandable range, well inside the 50% swing this Blueprint treats as the ceiling for Gate 4, and responds logically to price changes, with no sudden drops or recoveries that would suggest artificial demand or promotion-driven behaviour.

Pricing is calm. Adjustments are small, infrequent, and incremental. Sellers aren't aggressively undercutting each other, and there's no sign of panic or churn. This listing behaves the same way week after week.

## Competition and Amazon's Absence (Gates 5–6)

Amazon hasn't appeared on the Buy Box at all in the last 90 days, nowhere near the 30% Gate 5 threshold. The offer count sits at 6, under the Gate 6 ceiling of 10, and pricing moves in small, infrequent steps rather than aggressive undercutting. Sellers compete, but within a predictable, survivable range. Outcomes here are driven by normal market behaviour rather than intervention.

## Why This Product Works Long Term

This isn't a product that will impress on a spreadsheet or in a sourcing group. It won't generate excitement or urgency. What it offers is reliability. Because pricing is stable and competition is measured, repricing doesn't need constant attention, inventory decisions are easier to manage, and risk is spread over time rather than concentrated. This type of product rewards patience and consistency over speed and aggression.

## Core Lesson

Stability beats excitement over time. Most sustainable Amazon businesses are built on products like this, not the ones with the highest margins, but the ones that behave predictably. While others chase volatility and "perfect" deals, boring products quietly compound. This is the kind of listing where sellers look back and wish they'd scaled earlier, not the kind they regret entering.
