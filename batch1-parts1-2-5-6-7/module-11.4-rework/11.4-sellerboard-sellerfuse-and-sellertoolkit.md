# 11.4 Sellerboard and SellerToolKit

As you start shipping and selling regularly, you'll want more than the Buy List and your Amazon payout schedule to understand how your business is actually doing. A few tools are worth knowing about here.

### SellerToolKit: now connected directly to SourceSheets

SellerToolKit isn't something you bolt on separately anymore, it connects straight into your SourceSheets account under **Settings → SellerToolKit**. The integration runs both ways:

* **Import from SellerToolKit** lets you pull ASIN stock and sales data into SourceSheets. You'll need a token generated from SellerToolKit itself, under its Settings → Partner Link Tokens, then paste it in and connect.
* **Share with SellerToolKit** does the reverse, generating a token that lets SellerToolKit look up your supplier and buy-list cost details.

Neither side is switched on by default, you decide if and when to connect it. For a brand-new seller in your first month, there's no need to set this up yet, you don't have enough sales volume for it to tell you anything useful. Once you're shipping and selling consistently, it's worth connecting for proper stock, fee, and reimbursement tracking without doing it by hand.

### Sellerboard

Sellerboard is a separate profit and analytics dashboard many established sellers run alongside SourceSheets. It's not connected to your SourceSheets account, you'd set it up independently through Amazon's own reporting once you want that level of detail on ad spend, refunds, and true per-SKU profit.

### Repricers

Some established sellers also run a repricer alongside SourceSheets, a separate tool that automatically adjusts listing prices in response to competitor moves. Like Sellerboard, a repricer runs independently of SourceSheets. See 11.5 for what a repricer actually does, why you don't need one yet, and what to look for when you are ready.

### Why this waits

All three of these solve problems you don't have yet in your first month: you're focused on getting your first products in, sold, and paid out. Once that's happening consistently, come back to this page and connect what's actually useful to you.
