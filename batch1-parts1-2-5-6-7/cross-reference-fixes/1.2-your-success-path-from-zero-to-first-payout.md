# 1.2 Your Success Path: From Zero to First Payout

This Blueprint gives you a **simple, proven path** to go from:

👉 No business, no Amazon account, no experience
to
👉 Your first products purchased, shipped, sold: and your first payout received

### 🧭 Here's Exactly What Your Journey Looks Like

### 🏗️ Build Your Business

You'll start by putting the foundations in place.

This includes setting up your LTD company, your business bank account, your Amazon Seller Account, and the small number of tools you'll actually use.

Once these are in place, you're officially ready to begin.

### 🧠 Learn the Basics & Evaluate Products

Next, you'll learn how to identify profitable products using the right data.

We'll show you:

* What a "good" product looks like
* How to read the charts and sales data
* How to check restrictions & ungating
* How to use the SourceSheets ASIN Analyser
* How to use SourceSheets to filter opportunities fast

This gives you the skills to make safe, confident buying decisions.

### 🛍️ Make Your First Purchase

Using SourceSheets leads, your evaluation skills, and support from our staff, you'll find your first real opportunity.

Then you'll:

* Place your first order (We're partnered with Qogita to give you £100 credit towards your first stock)
* Add the products to your Amazon inventory
* Start tracking everything properly

This is the moment you become an active seller.

### 🚚 Send Your First Shipment to Amazon

Once your products arrive:

* You label and prepare your items
* You create your shipment in Seller Central
* You drop off with UPS or arrange collection
* Amazon receives, checks in, and stocks your products

Your store becomes live the moment your products become fulfilable.

### 💸 Make Sales & Get Your First Payout

When your products go live:

* You get your first sales
* You track your performance
* Amazon schedules your payout

Once your first payout lands, you've officially completed the Blueprint.

🎉 You unlock your free extra month of SourceSheets
(we verify your Milestones and add it automatically)

You've gone from zero → fully trading Amazon seller.

### Summary

In summary, your success path is:

1. Set up your business
2. Activate your Amazon account
3. Learn the basics
4. Evaluate products
5. Place your first order
6. Send your first shipment
7. Make your first sale
8. Receive your first payout

A clear, simple journey: and you'll be supported at every step.
