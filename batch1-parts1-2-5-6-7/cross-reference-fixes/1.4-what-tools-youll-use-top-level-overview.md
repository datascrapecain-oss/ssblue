# 1.4 What Tools You'll Use (Top-Level Overview)

To run a simple, successful Amazon FBA business, you only need a small handful of tools: and we introduce them exactly when you need them, not before.

Here's the top-level overview of the tools you'll be using throughout the Blueprint.

### 📄 SourceSheets

This is the core of your sourcing system.

You'll use SourceSheets to:

* find real product opportunities
* check basic profitability
* filter out bad products quickly
* access guided daily leads
* use tools like Ungate Search, Find a Product, and more

SourceSheets gives you clarity, not overwhelm: and it's where most sellers find their first winning products.

### 🔍 SourceSheets ASIN Analyser

This is your built-in analysis tool. There's nothing to choose and nothing to set up outside SourceSheets, it's already part of your account.

For any single product, it tells you:

* if you can sell it
* how fast it sells
* how profitable it is
* who else is selling it
* whether you're gated
* the full Keepa-style chart data

It helps you avoid mistakes and make confident buying decisions, whether you're checking a product from a sheet or an ASIN you've found elsewhere.

### 🛒 Amazon Seller Central

This is your Amazon management hub.

You'll use Seller Central to:

* create shipments
* add products
* track inbound stock
* view sales
* read performance notifications
* see your payout schedule

You'll only use a few areas of it at first: we show you exactly where to click.

### 🏢 Tide (Company Formation & Backup Bank)

We use Tide exclusively for:

* forming your LTD at a discounted rate
* receiving your £20 reward for completing their onboarding
* automatically opening a basic business account

This Tide account is not used as your main trading bank. It simply handles your company formation in the quickest, easiest way: and gives you a backup bank account if you ever need one.

### 🏦 Revolut Business (Your Trading Bank)

We're partnered with Revolut, who offer support and guidance to new sellers. Your business will run through Revolut:

* clean business banking
* smooth Amazon compatibility
* easy payouts
* direct SourceSheets support

You'll set this up early so you can trade properly and avoid future issues.

### 📦 Your Wholesale Supplier

Once you're ready to buy, you'll set up an account with a wholesale supplier. SourceSheets surfaces supplier stock through the Sourcing Library and Radar, so you're choosing from products already matched to Amazon listings, not searching blind. Look for:

* clear wholesale pricing
* proper Amazon-compatible invoices
* fast delivery
* consistent stock

This is where you'll place your first real order.

### 🧩 A Simple, Beginner-Friendly Stack

You don't need 10 apps. You don't need complicated dashboards. You just need these essentials: introduced at the right time:

* SourceSheets → find opportunities
* ASIN Analyser → analyse safely
* Seller Central → ship & sell
* Revolut → run your business
* Wholesale supplier → place your first order

As you grow, we'll show you optional extras like:

* repricers (PPP, SellerFuse)
* accounting partners (RTAccounting)

But right now?

This is all you need: nothing more.
