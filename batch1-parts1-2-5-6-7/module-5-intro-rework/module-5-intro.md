# Module 5: Your Essential Tools Setup

## Getting the ASIN Analyser Ready

Now that your business is legally formed, your banking is live, and your Amazon Seller account is fully verified, you're ready for the tool that powers your product research: the SourceSheets ASIN Analyser.

Every successful Amazon seller uses a profitability analysis tool. Without one, you're guessing. With one, you're running a real business.

## One Tool, Already Built In

Unlike older versions of this Blueprint, there's no separate sign-up, no trial to start, and no choice to make between two different products. The ASIN Analyser lives inside SourceSheets itself. If you've already got a SourceSheets account, you've already got it.

It gives you everything a standalone analysis tool would: profitability on every ASIN, fee and ROI breakdowns, real-time FBA and FBM competition, sales velocity estimates, hazmat and eligibility checks, and price and rank history. It's also the tool the rest of this Blueprint, the 7 Gates model in particular, is built around, so what you see here is exactly what you'll be using to evaluate every product going forward.

## What This Module Covers

Over the next few pages, we'll walk you through running your first analysis, setting up your Preferences (tax mode, buying criteria, and the tool you have selected for one-click analysis), and customising which panels you see so the interface shows you what matters and nothing else.

By the end, you'll know exactly how to read the ASIN Analyser and be ready to start evaluating real products.
