# 5.6 The Partner Directory: Trusted Services to Support Your Growth

The Partner Directory is a curated list of trusted companies, tools, and service providers that SourceSheets members commonly use as they scale. Instead of searching blindly for accountants, mentors, wholesalers, or support tools, the directory gives you a pre-vetted set of partners who already understand Amazon businesses and the workflows you're learning.

This page isn't something you need to use right now, but it's incredibly useful as you grow. As you build your Amazon business, you'll eventually need services like accounting, wholesale suppliers, business mentoring, and growth tools. The Partner Directory is where you'll find them.

### 🔍 What the Partner Directory Is For

It helps you quickly find:

* Wholesale suppliers
* Business mentors
* Accountants and VAT specialists
* Automation tools
* Financing partners
* Ungating assistance
* Repricer or analytics software

Every partner in this list is selected because they're already serving hundreds of Amazon sellers, and because they offer perks, discounts, or exclusive benefits to SourceSheets members.
