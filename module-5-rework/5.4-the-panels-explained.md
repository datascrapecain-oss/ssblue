# 5.4 The Panels Explained

Here's what each panel on the ASIN Analyser is actually telling you.

> 📸 **Screenshot needed:** a full analysis result showing all seven panels, scrollable or in one long capture, ideally on a real product where most panels have something worth pointing at.

### Profit Calculator

Sits at the top, next to the product summary. Enter your **Cost Price**, and it works out everything else against the current **Sale Price**: Profit, ROI, Total Fees, Referral Fee, FBA Fee, Break-even Sale Price, and Estimated Amazon Payout. Toggle between FBA and FBM, and between No VAT and VAT, to match how you'd actually sell it. To the right, a small table shows what profit and ROI would have looked like on average over the last 30, 90, and 180 days at the current price tier, useful for spotting a price that's temporarily inflated.

### Ranks & Prices

Current BSR, Buy Box price, Amazon's own price if they're on the listing, lowest FBA and FBM offers, how many times the Keepa-tracked BSR has dropped (each drop is a rough proxy for a sale), and total offer count. Switch between Current, 30, 90, and 180 day views to see whether today's numbers are typical or an outlier.

### Buy Box Analysis

Shows who has actually been winning the buy box, and how often, as a percentage over your selected time period. If Amazon holds it 100% of the time, that's a very different competitive picture to five sellers splitting it evenly. This is your Gate 5 and Gate 6 data from Module 7.

### Charts

Three charts stacked together:

* **Price & Sales Rank**: Amazon's price, FBA price, FBM price, Buy Box price, and Sales Rank, all plotted over time so you can see how they move together or against each other
* **Sales Per Month**: a rolling estimate of monthly unit sales
* **Offers and Reviews**: how offer count, star rating, and review count have moved over the same period

Switch the range between Day, Week, Month, Quarter, and All to zoom in on recent behaviour or see the full history.

### Variations

If the ASIN is part of a variation family (different sizes or colours of the same product, for example), this lists the sibling ASINs and their variation attribute, so you can check whether a different variant might be a better buy.

### Offers

Every seller currently on the listing: condition, price, and shipping cost, split by Live and Historical, and filterable to All, FBA only, or FBM only. The headline row shows the current Buy Box, Lowest FBA, and Lowest FBM price at a glance.

### Other Suppliers

This one has no equivalent in third-party tools, because it draws on SourceSheets' own supplier database. It shows you every other supplier SourceSheets tracks that also carries this exact ASIN, with their cost, the profit you'd make buying from them instead, and the difference against your current source. Worth checking every time: sometimes a better deal on the same product is sitting one supplier over.

### Fees & Logistics

Referral fee percentage, FBA pick and pack fee, variable closing fee where it applies, package dimensions, package weight, and which fulfilment method is currently winning the buy box. This is the detail behind the Profit Calculator's numbers, useful when a fee looks higher than you expected and you want to see exactly where it's coming from.
